# 11. Companion ambitions bind to additive authored world sites

Date: 2026-10-28

## Status

Proposed. The source foundation passes 57 distinct focused cases and six scoped type paths. Fixture comparisons preserve 75 existing site records and nine existing plans, excluding only measured generation duration. Normal conversation, physical discovery, route and restoration outcomes remain unverified.

## Context

Elara's public ambition names the desecrated Temple of the Morning, but the companion goal has no world address. Actual Mara play preserves her preparations and memories without supplying a restoration destination. The current Serara atlas identifies its existing temple with Parasism and Paras, The Bronze Kraken. Its five temple crypts and the bought Iolodidau sewer account do not establish Elara's temple.

## Decision

Add a distinct authored story site through the existing dungeon-site registry. This is new world content with explicit provenance, not a claim that an unrelated existing site always belonged to Elara.

- Give the public goal a versioned source ID, full world seed, fixed site path, public name and companion/goal identity. Keep the complete atlas seed distinct from the frozen uint32 path root.
- Select a land cell from canonical geography using a new named seed stream. Selection reads no player position, party, approval, inventory, discovered cells or quest state. It excludes existing sites, burg seats and river cells and requires a nearby real settlement. No suitable cell means no source; do not invent a fallback location.
- Append the new site after existing marker, temple, sewer and civilization sources. Existing site records, generated plans, names, histories, geometry and encounters remain unchanged. The new source begins with the established crypt/ruin-door scaffold for its undercroft; that does not establish a complete surface temple or a restored sanctuary.
- Carry its authored identity through the normal naming, entrance, discovery and runtime receipt paths. Keep old generation seeds and RNG draw counts intact. A new source must regenerate identically through its own full-seed receipt path.
- Public conversation may share a verified source address through existing quest, discovery and memory owners. Sharing a location grants no approval, recruitment, combat clear or restoration credit. Private ambitions remain private.
- Qualify the actual entrance and route through normal gameplay. Terrain and encounter difficulty are not changed to obtain a successful demonstration. Desecration and restoration consequences need their own real source state and played evidence.

## Consequences

The first implementation is a source foundation. The complete companion arc still requires believable responsibilities and relationships, earned consent, actual skills, independent opinions, persistent choices and consequences, and separation/reunion. Existing historical saves and unrelated authored/procedural sites remain evidence. Once a v1 address is observed or saved, future selection changes require a versioned source and an explicit continuity policy.
