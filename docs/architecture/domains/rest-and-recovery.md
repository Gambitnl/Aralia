## Recovery ownership

`src/hooks/actions/handleResourceActions.ts` owns rest eligibility, world events,
clock advancement and player messages. `characterReducer.ts` applies resource
changes. `RestModal.tsx` and `LongRestModal.tsx` explain costs before confirmation.
`actionHandlers.ts` routes the modal choices through the full gameplay handlers.
A direct recovery reducer action does not perform overnight world work.

A short rest takes one hour. The player selects Hit Dice to spend; excess healing
does not refund a die. The handler rolls healing, records the completed rest and
advances the clock. Most spell slots and exhaustion need a long rest instead.
`restTiming.ts` shares the existing one-hour duration, three-rest cap and two-hour
spacing between preview and handler. The cap uses the rest's finish day; spacing
is measured from the previous finish, including negative game-epoch timestamps.
The dialog displays the handler's timing refusal and disables commitment.

A long rest takes eight hours at the current location. At least one member must
have 1 HP. Members at 0 HP gain no benefits. The handler rejects an empty or wholly
incapacitated party before overnight side effects. The last-rest timestamp records
completion, followed by a 16-hour wait before the next long rest can start.

Provision accounting uses total quantities across all carried stacks. The party
consumes one ration and one water unit per member only when there is enough for
everyone. Eating clears the existing travel hardship conditions. A member who
remains starving cannot recover HP. The 2014 and 2024 rules retain their separate
Hit Dice and exhaustion recovery. Planar restrictions can deny individual members
benefits. Persistent consequences outside those explicit rules are retained.

Rest dialogs hold confirmation open while asynchronous work completes. Repeated
clicks cannot start another rest from the same displayed state. Background input
and passive seconds pause during rest and lodging decisions. Explicit wait and
rest actions still advance the world and town routines. Waiting grants no recovery
and consumes no rest provisions. Players can choose 1–24 hours independently of
the recovery cooldown, or use the displayed eligibility shortcut.

Both preview and action routing check the source water environment before
waiting. A submerged party cannot skip hours in place through the rest dialog.
A private property's sleep restriction is separate from environment safety.

the active baseline, proposed spell IDs, campaign edition and only the relevant
verified spell levels. `preparedSpells` remains the casting list while editing.
Reset discards only the plan. Normal saves retain both lists; no save migration
rewrites a character's existing active choices.

`spellPreparation.ts` validates the existing class list, actual class level,
available slots and learned wizard spells. The handler obtains levels from the
canonical spell manifest before the reducer edits a plan. Direct internal toggles
without verified levels cannot change preparation. Racial locked preparation is
retained. A stale baseline or changed edition leaves the plan unapplied.

known/level-up casters do not gain daily replacement controls. Broader subclass,
always-prepared and multiclass list ownership remain outside this qualification.

Only eligible members in an accepted `LONG_REST` commit valid plans. Short rests,
cooldown refusal, planar denial and 0 HP retain the current spells and pending
choices. The 2014 capacity calculation uses its ability modifier and class level.
